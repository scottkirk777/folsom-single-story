Where single-story homes sold in Folsom

About half of Folsom home sales were single-story: 383 of 762. Broadstone (60%), Empire Ranch (58%) and Willow Creek (56%) had the highest share among non-55+ neighborhoods with at least 20 sales. All 46 sales in Regency at Folsom Ranch, a 55+ community, were single-story.

What to check in a single-story home

One story is a start. If you plan to stay a long time, check these too:

  • A step-free way in, from the garage or the front door.
  • Hallways and doors wide enough for a walker.
  • A walk-in shower, or room to add one.
  • A flat lot. Some hillside lots have steps up to the front door.
  • Laundry inside the house, not in the garage.

Common questions

Which Folsom neighborhoods have the most single-story homes?
About half of Folsom single-family sales were single-story: 383 of 762. Regency at Folsom Ranch, a 55+ community, was all single-story (46 sales). Among other neighborhoods with at least 20 sales, Broadstone had the highest share at 60%, then Empire Ranch at 58% and Willow Creek at 56%. By count, the most single-story sales were in Regency at Folsom Ranch (46), Empire Ranch (43) and Willow Creek (36), from MetroList sales in the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026. The table ranks every Folsom neighborhood by share of sales that were single-story.
Do single-story homes cost more in Folsom?
Per square foot, yes. The median single-story sale was $416 per square foot, against $340 for two-story homes. But single-story homes are smaller, so the median price was lower: $699,000 against $849,000. Source: MetroList, the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026.
How fast do single-story homes sell in Folsom?
A little faster. The median single-story home sold in 15 days on market, against 18 days for two-story homes. Source: MetroList, the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026.
Is there a 55+ community with single-story homes in Folsom?
Yes. Regency at Folsom Ranch, by Toll Brothers, south of Highway 50, is marketed as a 55+ active-adult community with single-story designs. See downsizing at 55+.
