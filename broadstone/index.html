Taxes, districts and HOA in Broadstone

Sales data updated Nov 4, 2026 · Tax bills read Nov 4, 2026

Broadstone is north of Highway 50. Its homes pay landscape and lighting district charges, $40.22 to $164.98 per district depending on the unit. The sample bill on Rundgren Way had two of them, $207.82 in all. Broadstone Unit 3, the Hillcrest area, also pays CFD 12, a maintenance district.

The base property tax north of Highway 50 is 1.0831% on the 2026-27 county bills. HOA dues: $187 a month median, 23% of sales. The Folsom cost to own guide adds it up for any price.

District charges come from one real 2026-27 Sacramento County tax bill for a home in each neighborhood. Mello-Roos is set by home size and type, so a bigger or smaller home in the same neighborhood can pay more or less. Every sample bill is listed line by line in the sample bill register.

What to check on a single-story home here

  • Entry: count the steps from the driveway and the garage.
  • Shower: a walk-in or low-curb shower, or room to add one.
  • Yard: a size you want to keep up.
  • Price per foot: single-story homes here sold for more per square foot, partly because they tend to be smaller. Compare homes of a similar size.
