Sources and method · updated November 2026

Where every number comes from

Every figure comes from MetroList, the City of Folsom, Sacramento County or another source linked here. When a number isn't checked yet, the page shows a dashed box instead.

Sales data

MetroList sold single-family homes in ZIP 95630, the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026: 762 sales. Single-story means the listing reports one story. Regency at Folsom Ranch counts sales MetroList lists as a senior community in Folsom Ranch. Neighborhoods come from the MetroList subdivision field. About 6 in 10 sales had no usable name there, so most of those were placed by their assessor map page, matched to labeled sales on the same or a nearby page. Treat neighborhood lines as close, not exact. A share needs at least 10 sales, and a median at least 5 sales of that type.

Single-story and two-story

Each listing's stories field in MetroList. Price per square foot uses the close price and the listed living area. Days on market is the MetroList figure on the sold listing.

55+ communities

The MetroList senior community field, then each community's own marketing or listing pages, linked on the downsizing page. Sales, median price, days on market and HOA dues for each community come from the same MetroList sales.

Prop 19

The rules for movers 55 and older come from the California State Board of Equalization, which runs Prop 19 for the state. The claim form is BOE-19-B, filed with the county assessor. Ask your CPA how it applies to you.

How the sales were counted

Pulled from MetroList on Nov 4, 2026. Included: sold single-family listings in the City of Folsom, ZIP 95630, that closed from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026. That is 762 MLS numbers, each counted once. Six homes sold twice in the year, and both sales count. Prices are close prices.

Neighborhoods: 287 sales had a subdivision name that matched a neighborhood. 328 had a blank or unmatched name and were placed by their county assessor map page, using the neighborhood most labeled sales on that page, or on the nearest labeled page in the same map book. The other 147 could not be placed with confidence and are grouped as Other Folsom neighborhoods.

Based on information from MetroList Services, Inc. All information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed and should be independently verified.
