Can you buy a single-story home in Folsom for under $700,000?

Yes, about half of them sold for less. The median single-story sale in Folsom was $699,000, across 383 single-story sales in the 12 months from Nov 1, 2025 to Oct 31, 2026. Willow Creek, at $692,500, and Broadstone, at $697,000, were the named neighborhoods with the most single-story sales under that mark.

  • Median single-story price: $699,000, against $849,000 for two-story.
  • What $700,000 buys: about 1,680 square feet at Folsom's median $416 a square foot.
  • Speed: the median single-story home sold in 15 days.

How much house does $700,000 buy?

Price per square foot is higher for single-story homes, so the same budget buys a little less space. Citywide, single-story homes sold for $416 a square foot, against $340 for two-story.

Use this as a starting point only. A remodeled home on a big lot sells for more per foot than an original one on a small lot.

Three ways to stretch a $700,000 budget

  1. Look past the big names. Sales outside the named neighborhoods had the lowest single-story median, $590,000. Ask me for the streets where those sold.
  2. Check the yearly cost, not just the price. Folsom Ranch homes carry Mello-Roos. A lower price can come with a higher tax bill. The Folsom cost to own guide adds it up by neighborhood.
  3. Watch days on market. Willow Creek and Broadstone single-story homes sold in a median of 10 and 8 days. Be ready to write an offer the week a good one lists.
