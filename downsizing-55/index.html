Prop 19 for movers 55 and older

  • Who: a homeowner 55 or older. This section covers only the 55+ rule.
  • What moves: the taxable value of your main home, to a replacement main home anywhere in California.
  • How many times: up to three.
  • When: buy the replacement within two years before or after you sell.
  • Price test: up to 100% of the old home's sale price if you buy first, 105% within a year after the sale, 110% within two years. Above that, the difference is added.
  • Form: BOE-19-B, filed with the county assessor where the new home is. File within three years of buying the replacement for full relief back to the purchase date.

Severely disabled homeowners and disaster victims have their own rules and forms: BOE-19-D for disability and BOE-19-V for a Governor-declared disaster, which has no limit on the number of transfers. See the Sacramento County Assessor's Prop 19 page.

Rules: State Board of Equalization, Prop 19. Talk to your CPA before you sell.

Common questions

Are there 55+ communities in Folsom?
Yes. Regency at Folsom Ranch, by Toll Brothers, south of Highway 50, is marketed as a 55+ active-adult community with single-story designs. Diamond Glen, north of Highway 50, is an older 55+ community of single-story homes built 1997 to 1999. The table on this page has the sales numbers for both.
Can I keep my low property tax if I move to Folsom after 55?
Prop 19 lets a homeowner 55 or older move the taxable value of their main home to a replacement main home anywhere in California, up to three times. The replacement must be bought within two years before or after the sale. If it costs more than the allowed limit, the difference is added. You file form BOE-19-B with the county assessor. Confirm your case with your CPA.
What if the new home costs more than the one I sold?
The value test depends on timing: up to 100% of the old home's sale price if you buy before you sell, 105% if you buy within the first year after, and 110% within the second year. Above that, the extra is added to your taxable value.
